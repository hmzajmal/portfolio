Operating principles

## What goes into my work.

### Research over assumption.

Every screen starts with a real user problem. Interviews, support tickets, session replays. No designing in a vacuum.

### Ship the boring parts.

Empty states, error states, edge cases, design systems. The unglamorous parts are where the product actually lives.

### Move the metric.

Activation, retention, conversion. If the design does not move a number, it is decoration. Outcomes over aesthetics.

### Engineers in the room.

Best designs are co-authored. I pair with engineering from kickoff so what ships matches what was specced.
